Privacy Policy

Brainiacs: Math & Puzzle

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Brainiacs: Math & Puzzle ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information Collection

The App does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or external servers. No account registration is required.

2. Local Data Storage

Game scores, progress, statistics, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer or shared with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data remains on your device. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy if the App's features or data practices change. Updates will be published here with a revised date.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: brainiacs.game@gmail.com
